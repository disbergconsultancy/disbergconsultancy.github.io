Sphere Runner

Concept prototype. Swipe left/right to switch lane, up to jump, down to slide (or slam down in the air).

How to play
Low block: jump
Floating bar: slide under
Wall: switch lane
Moving block: time your pass
Glowing floor: jump across
Long block: take the ramp and run on top
Coin · close call = bonus
Power-ups: magnet · shield · orbit · x2
Boss (end of each world): it waits on the horizon and throws things at you. Catch up and run past it to beat it.

Keep coins and close calls coming for a combo. Hold your finger after a jump to float. Switch lanes in the air for a quick air dash.
